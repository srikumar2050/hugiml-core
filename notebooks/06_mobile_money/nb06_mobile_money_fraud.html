Fraud Pattern Analysis

Balance Manipulation Patterns

Destination-Amount Ratio Anomaly (Top Pattern):

Pattern dest_to_amount_ratio=[-0.25,0.92) (+2.65 coefficient) detects when destination balance change doesn't match transfer amount - the classic PaySim fraud indicator from Lopez-Rojas et al. (2016).

  • Normal Transfer: dest_to_amount_ratio ≈ 1.0 (destination increases by transfer amount)
  • Fraud Pattern: dest_to_amount_ratio ≈ 0.0 (destination unchanged despite transfer)
  • Research Alignment: Matches published PaySim fraud signature

Origin Account Drainage

  • newbalanceOrig=[0,369) (+2.36) - Origin drained to near-zero
  • origin_significant_drop indicator - Balance drops >70%
  • Fraud Mechanism: Attacker drains compromised account, destination unchanged

Large Balance Targeting

  • oldbalanceOrg=[94K,50M) (+2.49) - High-value accounts targeted
  • oldbalanceOrg=[29K,94K) (+1.57) - Medium-high value accounts
  • Fraud Strategy: Attackers target accounts with substantial balances

Transaction Type Risk

  • TRANSFER: 0.19% fraud rate (10x baseline)
  • CASH_OUT: 0.15% fraud rate (elevated risk)
  • PAYMENT/CASH_IN/DEBIT: <0.01% fraud rate (low risk)
