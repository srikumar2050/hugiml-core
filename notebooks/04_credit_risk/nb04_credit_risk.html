01 Temporal Split Guidance Best Practice
Credit models require temporal holdouts. A purely random 70/30 split leaks future vintage information into training, inflating out-of-sample AUC and masking covariate shift. The German Credit dataset has no explicit date column; this notebook uses a stratified random split as an illustrative stand-in.

Production Best Practice

  • 📅 Sort by application date; use most recent 20–30% as OOT holdout
  • 🔄 Use TimeSeriesSplit for time-respecting CV
  • 📊 Monitor PSI on each new production window (see §9)
  • 🔀 Separate calibration holdout from test holdout

04 Fair Lending Analysis (ECOA) All groups within ±20%
ECOA §701 prohibits discrimination based on age, sex, marital status, and other protected bases. Approval-rate disparity exceeding ±20% from the overall rate triggers mandatory investigation. personal_status in the German Credit coding conflates gender with marital status — a prototypical prohibited-basis proxy removed in §05.
