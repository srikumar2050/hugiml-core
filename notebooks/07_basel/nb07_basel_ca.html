Regulatory Context

Basel III Framework & US Stress Testing

  • Basel III CET1: Minimum 4.5% Common Equity Tier 1 ratio + 2.5% conservation buffer
  • Leverage Ratio: Minimum 3% supplementary leverage requirement
  • LCR: Liquidity Coverage Ratio >100% for 30-day stress scenario
  • NSFR: Net Stable Funding Ratio >100% for stable funding
  • CCAR/DFAST: Annual stress testing for banks >$100B assets

Dataset Characteristics

  • Size: 5,000 banks (4,600 healthy, 400 undercapitalized)
  • Features: CET1 ratio, Tier 1 ratio, leverage ratio, LCR, NSFR, NPL ratio, concentration risk
  • Stress Scenario: Economic downturn with 2% GDP decline, unemployment +3%, housing -15%
  • Model: HUG-IML B=10, L=1, G=5e-4, topK=100
