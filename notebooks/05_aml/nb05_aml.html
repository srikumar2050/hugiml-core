Regulatory Context

FATF 40 Recommendations & FinCEN SAR Requirements

Key Regulatory Requirements:

  • FATF Recommendation 10: Customer Due Diligence and record-keeping for transactions exceeding designated threshold
  • FATF Recommendation 16: Wire transfer screening and monitoring for suspicious patterns
  • FinCEN SAR Filing: Suspicious Activity Report required within 30 days for transactions $5,000+ showing potential structuring
  • Bank Secrecy Act (BSA): Monitoring for cash transactions $10,000+ and patterns designed to evade reporting
  • USA PATRIOT Act Section 314(a): Information sharing for terrorist financing and money laundering detection

Dataset Characteristics

Synthetic AML Transaction Dataset

  • Size: 10,000 transactions (9,500 legitimate, 500 suspicious)
  • Fraud Rate: 5% (realistic for production AML systems)
  • Transaction Types: Wire transfers, ACH, checks, cash transactions
  • Key Features: Transaction amounts, velocity (24h activity), account age, structuring score, customer type, country risk
  • Structuring Pattern: Transactions clustered at $9,000-$9,950 range (just below $10K reporting threshold)
  • High-Risk Indicators: Offshore jurisdictions (VG, KY, PA), unusual transaction timing, new accounts with high activity

AML Risk Pattern Analysis

Structuring Detection (Primary Indicator)

  • Amount Range $9,000-$9,950: Three distinct patterns (#2, #6, #10) capture transactions just below $10K CTR threshold
  • Structuring Score 0.73-1.0: Highest coefficient (+7.20) - composite metric combining amount clustering and velocity
  • BSA Compliance: Pattern directly addresses 31 CFR 1010.314 anti-structuring provisions

High-Risk Jurisdiction Indicators

  • Offshore Countries: Transactions involving Virgin Islands (VG), Cayman Islands (KY), Panama (PA)
  • FATF Grey/Black List: Pattern #3 flags jurisdictions with weak AML controls
  • Enhanced Due Diligence: Triggers additional review per FATF Recommendation 19

Velocity & Timing Patterns

  • High Transaction Velocity: 9-16 transactions in 24 hours indicates potential layering (money laundering stage 2)
  • Off-Hours Activity: Transactions at 12am-3am and 10pm-midnight correlate with suspicious behavior
  • New Account Red Flag: Accounts <20 days old with high activity show elevated risk

SAR Reporting Framework

FinCEN Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Filing

Automatic SAR Triggers:

  • Any transaction matching patterns #1-3 (structuring score >0.73, amounts $9K-$9.95K, high-risk country)
  • Combinations of 3+ secondary indicators (velocity, timing, new account)
  • Risk score >0.95 based on model probability output

SAR Timeline: File within 30 calendar days of initial detection per 31 CFR 1020.320

Model Governance & Monitoring

Model Card: AML Transaction Monitor v1.0

Model ID: aml-structuring-v1.0.0

Model Type: HUG-IML Pattern-Based Classifier

Model Complexity: 72 single-feature patterns

Intended Use: Real-time AML transaction monitoring and SAR identification

Training Data: Synthetic transaction dataset (10,000 samples, 5% suspicious rate)

Performance: AUC 0.9990, Precision 0.95, Recall 0.97

Hyperparameters: B=10 bins, L=1 (single features), G=5e-4 regularization

Regulatory Alignment: FATF 40 Recommendations, FinCEN SAR requirements, BSA compliance

Limitations: (1) Trained on synthetic data - production requires validation on institution-specific transactions, (2) Structuring patterns may evolve - quarterly retraining recommended, (3) Cannot detect sophisticated trade-based money laundering without additional trade finance features

Ethical Considerations: High precision (0.95) minimizes false positives affecting legitimate customers. All 72 patterns fully explainable for customer inquiries and regulatory audits. Model does not use protected characteristics (race, religion, national origin) per ECOA requirements.

Monitoring Protocol: Daily pattern drift detection, weekly false positive rate tracking, monthly SAR filing statistics review, quarterly model retraining with new confirmed suspicious cases.
