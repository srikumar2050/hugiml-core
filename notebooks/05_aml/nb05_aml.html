03 BSA Demographic Review & Pattern Pruning PatternEditor
Geographic patterns retained. Patterns referencing country and high_risk_country map directly to FinCEN high-risk jurisdiction flags (FATF Recommendations 10/12) and are required considerations — they are not removed.
Low-signal age patterns removed. BSA / FinCEN guidance discourages demographic profiling as a primary suspicious-activity indicator. Two customer_age patterns with weak signal (|coef| < 0.20, support < 11%) were removed to reduce disparate-impact exposure.
